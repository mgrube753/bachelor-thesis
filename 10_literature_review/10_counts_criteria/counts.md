# Counts of Examined Papers in the Initial Literature Review

## Search Methods

Two search methods were used to gather papers for the literature review:

### 1. Elicit

- Focusing LLMs: 16  
- Question Generation / Bloom’s Taxonomy: 16  
- Evaluation of LLMs: 16  

Elicit was used to search for papers using a prompt for each topic. The first 16 results were selected for each topic, ensuring a diverse range of papers. This kind of search was a useful way to quickly gather relevant literature, but several papers were displayed e.g. in its pre-print version, instead of the final published version. This was not a problem for the literature review, but it is worth noting that the Elicit search results may not always reflect the most up-to-date or final versions of papers.

### 2. Google Scholar

- Focusing LLMs: 40  
- Question Generation / Bloom’s Taxonomy: 60  
- Evaluation of LLMs: 60  

Google Scholar was used to search for papers using a query for each topic. The first 40 results for the focusing LLMs and the first 60 results for the other two topics were selected, based on relevance to the topic.
